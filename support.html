Questions

When will DishLift be on the App Store?

There is no release date and no store listing yet. The status is “coming soon.” Email info@tjnai.ai if you want a note when it is available.

What does the app do?

You photograph a recipe you already like. DishLift reads it, lets you correct the read, and can rework it for more protein, less sodium, or less sugar. It can also make an AI image of the dish, labeled as generated, and keep recipes in a private cookbook with chapters.

Is this medical or dietary advice?

No. Suggestions are general cooking ideas. They are not a diagnosis, a treatment, or a meal plan. Nutrition numbers are estimates. If you are under a clinician’s care, or you cook around an allergy, use a qualified professional and read labels yourself.

Which changes can I ask for?

More protein, less sodium, or less sugar, one at a time. The aim is to keep the dish recognizable and to show what changed. DishLift does not offer disease-specific meal plans.

What happens to a recipe photo?

The design sends the photo to a Supabase backend, which calls Anthropic’s Claude to read the recipe. Details, including what stays on the device, are in the privacy policy. To ask for a deletion, email info@tjnai.ai.
